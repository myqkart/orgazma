# content.md — Content Bible

All interface copy for the site, plus the voice it should be written in. Anything not listed here should be written in the same voice.

## 1. Voice & Tone
- **Who it's for:** adult women, confident and curious, who read erotica on purpose and without apology.
- **Feeling:** unhurried, knowing, warm, a little dangerous. A friend leaning in to say something wicked.
- **Style rules:**
  - Tease, don't shout. Short sentences. Space between them.
  - Speak to her directly ("you", "your"), and put her pleasure and choice at the center.
  - Sensory over anatomical in the interface: heat, ache, breath, hunger, pulse, skin, slow, close, want.
  - Permission-giving: she is allowed to want this, take her time, and keep it to herself.
  - Never crude, never mocking, never male-gaze. No shame language.
- **Avoid:** exclamation marks, emojis, clickbait, pressure ("hurry", "don't miss out").
- **The explicit material lives in the stories.** The interface sets the mood and then gets out of the way.

- All stories must involve adult characters only; the footer line is part of the brand promise.

- Keep the rating meta tags and age gate in place on every deployment.
